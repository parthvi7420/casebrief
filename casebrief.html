CaseBrief

Organizing evidence after an online fraud incident

1. Unorganized Evidence

Paste a message/chat, or log a transaction. URLs inside pasted text are extracted automatically.

2. Information Extraction

Dates, amounts, UPI IDs, phone numbers, emails and URLs pulled from each evidence item.

3. Chronological Timeline

Events derived from evidence, ordered by time. Each card shows which modules fired.

4. Missing Information

Required fields that are still empty.

5. Contradiction Detection

Conflicting facts across evidence items.

6. Redaction

Sensitive identifiers are masked by default. Explicit reveal only.

7. Incident Report

Local-first prototype — nothing here leaves the browser.
